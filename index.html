About the band

Five of us, one big 80s night out

The Hedge are a five-piece band based on the Wirral, playing the big pop and rock songs of the 80s, played properly live with real vocal harmonies.

Drums, keys, bass, lead guitar and vocals, so those synth lines, guitar hooks and choruses sound the way you remember them.

  • Vocals
  • Lead guitar
  • Keys
  • Bass
  • Drums

What we play

Songs you'll be singing on the way home

80s pop and rock with vocal harmonies, plus a few favourites from beyond. Expect a set that fills the dance floor and gets the whole room singing along, with songs by:

  • Queen
  • U2
  • Supertramp
  • David Gray
  • Aztec Camera
  • …and more
